Economic / market normalization

USD per litre = local price ÷ local currency per USD. Fuel-only USD/100 km = USD/litre × 8 litres/100 km. This excludes wages, maintenance and other costs. NGN applies to Lagos/Abuja; ZAR to Cape Town.

City Intelligence · component contributions

Weights: mobility 30%, commercial 20%, environment 20%, market 15%, direction 15%. Displayed cells are weighted contributions summing to the score. Formula v2_demo: mobility = 100×(1−congestion); commercial = services/100k ÷400×100; environment = 100−2×PM2.5; market = 100−1000×seven-day FX coefficient of variation; direction = 50−500×congestion change. Each component is clipped to 0–100. Constants are illustrative policy choices, not validated scientific thresholds. All five inputs and ≥28 days are required; no silent reweighting.

Uncertainty experiment · remove air quality and trend

Withholding these two components reduces weighted coverage to 65% and suppresses the score. Missing does not mean zero. Population ±20% changes services-per-capita by −16.7% / +25%.
